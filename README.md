# Hi, I’m Lateef.

I build content systems and AI-assisted workflows for marketing teams and agencies. My work brings together content strategy, search research, editorial quality control, and practical automation.

## What I build

- **Content production systems** that bring research, briefs, drafting, and editorial review into a repeatable workflow.
- **Editorial tools** that check drafts against a client’s positioning, brief, and quality standards.
- **SEO and research workflows** that surface changes, opportunities, and evidence for teams to act on.
- **Reusable skills and utilities** for the research, writing, and review tasks behind the work.

I use this profile to document the systems and tools I use, with examples and context on how they work.
